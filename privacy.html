Privacy Policy — Phoenix Clipper

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Phoenix Clipper is a personal automation tool that uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

Via Google OAuth, the app requests the following YouTube scopes for the owner's own account only:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to the owner's channel
  • youtube.readonly — read the owner's channel info
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read the owner's analytics

How data is used

Data is used solely to automate video uploads to the owner's own YouTube channel. No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device only.
